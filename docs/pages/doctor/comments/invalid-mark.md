A suggestion's `mark` is `track`, a red strike and a green insert, or `pen`, a hand-drawn strike with the new words written above. Any other value draws as the document's default.

::incorrect
```md
To :suggestion[sign in]{from="log in" mark="marker"}, enter your email.
```
::

::correct
```md
To :suggestion[sign in]{from="log in" mark="pen"}, enter your email.
```
::
