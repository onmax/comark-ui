## What it does

The skill shapes research, a ranked shortlist, a comparison, or a plan into one page with a fixed shape:

- the **answer** in two sentences, with the decisions you have to make as yes/no questions
- **one picture per section**, picked by the shape of what it answers: a bubble chart, a bar chart, a flowchart of the order, or a short table
- the **detail folded** in accordions, the same parts in every item
- **what the agent didn't verify**, kept open at the end

Every fact lives in one section, so the page never says the same number twice.

## When to use it

Ask once the research is done, such as "Write up which modules we should migrate first" or "Turn this plan into a report I can approve." Your agent loads it when you have to choose between options or approve a plan. To be sure, say "Use the report skill."

Choose by what you have:

- Options to compare, or a plan to approve: this skill.
- Findings about code, a design, or a system that already exists: [Review document](/skills/review-document).
- A question about how something works: [Visual explanation](/skills/visual-explanation).

## It's working if

- Reading only the answer and its callout tells you what the agent recommends and what you have to decide.
- Each section opens with one sentence that says what to look at, then its picture.
- The numbers the answer rests on are in a chart or a table column, and table cells stay a line or two long.
- Nothing you decide on is folded, and everything you would only check is.
- The page ends with what the agent didn't verify and where its numbers came from.
